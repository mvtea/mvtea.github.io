**Hi!** I'm Mace, and I test software for space telescopes.

I'm a systems engineer at the [Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI)](https://www.stsci.edu/), supporting integration and testing for the [Webb (JWST)](https://www.jwst.nasa.gov/) and [Roman](https://roman.gsfc.nasa.gov/) ground systems and leading test automation efforts for the Integration & Test team.

I'm an astrophysicist by training, with B.A.s in physics and astronomy from Wesleyan University. While at Wesleyan, I [researched black hole X-ray binaries and strong gravitational lensing](https://mvtea.github.io/research/), [managed the student observatory](https://mvtea.github.io/outreach/), and [taught telescope operation and data science for astronomy](https://mvtea.github.io/teaching/).
